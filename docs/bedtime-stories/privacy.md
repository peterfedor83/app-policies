# Privacy Policy

**Bedtime Stories**  
Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy describes how the Bedtime Stories iOS app (“the App”) handles information. We designed the App so that a child’s details stay on the device and are never sent to us.

## 1. Who we are

The App is published by **adipro** (Peter Fedor).  
Contact: [peter.fedor@gmail.com](mailto:peter.fedor@gmail.com)

## 2. Information stored on your device

The App may store the following **only on the device**, using iOS UserDefaults. This data is **not uploaded** to our servers:

- Child’s first name (optional, used to personalise stories)
- Child’s gender (used to choose wording in the story)
- Language preference
- Story progress (which day’s story is unlocked)
- Playback preference (background music on/off)
- Whether the full library has been unlocked
- A flag that the premium-voice tip was dismissed

Deleting the App removes this data.

## 3. Information we do not collect

We do not create user accounts. We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. We do not use analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or advertising networks.

## 4. Story downloads

To play a story, the App downloads story text files from our content server:

`https://opc-vm3.adipro.eu/stories`

The request includes only the story day number and language code (for example `1_en.json.gz`). It does **not** include the child’s name, gender, or any other profile data. Personalisation happens on the device after the file is downloaded.

The server will typically see a standard network request (IP address, device/user-agent) as with any HTTPS download. We do not use this to identify a child.

## 5. Purchases

Unlocking the full library is a one-time In-App Purchase processed by **Apple**. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to the transaction: [https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## 6. Speech and audio

Narration uses Apple’s built-in **AVSpeechSynthesizer** (text-to-speech) on the device. Spoken text is not sent to us. Voice quality depends on the voices installed on the device (Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices).

Background music is a sound file bundled with the App and is played locally.

## 7. Children

The App is intended for families and young children. A child’s name is optional and stays on the device. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children on our servers. If you believe we have received such information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not share profile data with third parties. Apple provides the App Store, payments, and on-device speech. Our content host delivers story files as described above.

## 9. Your choices

- You can leave the child’s name empty.
- You can change or clear the name and other settings in the App at any time.
- You can delete the App to remove local data.
- You can restore a previous purchase with Apple’s Restore Purchases control.

## 10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.
